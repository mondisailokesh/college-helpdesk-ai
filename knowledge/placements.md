# Placements

## Does the college have a Placement Cell?

Yes.

The college has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that assists students with internships, training programs and campus placements.

---

## What is the role of the Placement Cell?

The Placement Cell:

- Organizes campus recruitment drives
- Conducts placement training
- Provides career guidance
- Helps students prepare for interviews
- Connects students with companies
- Shares internship opportunities

---

Placement training usually starts from the second or third year depending on the department.
