# Academics

## Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75%.

Students with attendance below 75% may not be eligible to write semester examinations unless permitted by university regulations.

---

## Working Days

Classes are conducted from Monday to Saturday.

Sunday is a holiday.

---

## Class Timings

First Hour : 9:00 AM

Last Hour : 4:30 PM

---

## Internal Examinations

Internal examinations are conducted twice every semester.

Assignments and quizzes also contribute to internal marks.

---

## Semester Examinations

Semester examinations are conducted at the end of every semester.

Students should carry their hall ticket and ID card.

---

## Bonafide Certificate

Students can obtain a bonafide certificate from the administration office.

Applications may also be submitted through the ERP portal.

---

## Transfer Certificate

Students should submit a written request to obtain a Transfer Certificate.

All dues must be cleared before the certificate is issued.

---

## Academic Support

Faculty members conduct doubt clarification sessions regularly.

Special remedial classes are arranged for slow learners.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum attendance?

75%.

### Can I get a bonafide certificate online?

Yes, through the ERP portal if available.

### Where can I collect my bonafide certificate?

Administration Office.

### How many internal exams are conducted?

Two.

### When are semester exams conducted?

At the end of every semester.
