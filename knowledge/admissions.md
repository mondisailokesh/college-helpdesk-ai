# Admissions

## Eligibility

Students must satisfy university eligibility criteria.

---

## Documents Required

- SSC Marks Memo
- Intermediate Marks Memo
- Transfer Certificate
- Study Certificates
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport Size Photographs

Step 2

Document Verification.

Step 3

Fee Payment.

Step 4

Admission Confirmation.
