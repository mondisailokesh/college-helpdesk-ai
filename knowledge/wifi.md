# Campus WiFi

## Does the college provide WiFi?

Yes.

The college provides WiFi connectivity across academic buildings, laboratories, library and selected campus areas for educational purposes.

---

## Who can use the campus WiFi?

Registered students, faculty members and authorized staff members can use the campus WiFi.

---

## Is WiFi available throughout the campus?

WiFi is available in most academic blocks, laboratories, library and common student areas.

---

## Is WiFi available in classrooms?

Yes.

Many classrooms have WiFi coverage for academic activities.

---

## Is WiFi available in laboratories?

Yes.

Computer laboratories have internet access for practical sessions.

---

## Is WiFi available in the library?

Yes.

Students can access WiFi while studying in the library.

---

## Is WiFi available in the hostel?

Yes.

Hostel WiFi is available according to hostel policies.

---

## How do I connect to the college WiFi?

Students should:

1. Enable WiFi on their device.
2. Select the college WiFi network.
3. Login using the provided credentials.
4. Accept the usage policy if required.

---

## What credentials are required?

Students generally use their registered username and password provided by the college.

---

## Can I use my student ID as the username?

Depending on the college configuration, the username may be the student ID or official email ID.

---

## What should I do if I forget my WiFi password?

Students should contact the IT Support Cell or reset the password through the student portal if available.

---

## Can I change my WiFi password?

Yes.

Password changes follow the IT department procedures.

---

## Is WiFi free?

Yes.

Campus WiFi is provided for academic purposes without additional charges.

## Can I connect multiple devices?

The number of devices depends on the college network policy.

## Is there a daily usage limit?

Internet usage policies may include bandwidth or usage limits.

## What should I do if WiFi is slow?

Students should:

- Reconnect to the network.
- Restart the device.
- Move closer to the access point.
- Contact IT Support if the problem continues.

---

## What should I do if I cannot connect?

Check:

- Username
- Password
- WiFi settings
- Network availability

If the problem continues, contact the IT Help Desk.
