# Faculty

## Who are the faculty members?

Faculty members include Professors, Associate Professors, Assistant Professors and Laboratory Instructors who teach and guide students.

---

## What is the role of a faculty member?

Faculty members teach courses, conduct laboratory sessions, guide projects, mentor students and evaluate academic performance.

---

## Who is the Head of the Department (HOD)?

The Head of the Department is responsible for managing academic activities, faculty members, laboratories and departmental administration.

---

## What does the HOD do?

The HOD:

- Supervises faculty
- Monitors academics
- Approves departmental activities
- Supports student development
- Coordinates university requirements

---

## Can students meet the HOD?

Yes.

Students may meet the HOD during office hours or by prior appointment.

---

## What is a faculty mentor?

A faculty mentor is assigned to guide students throughout their academic program.

---

## What does a mentor help with?

Mentors help students with:

- Academic performance
- Attendance
- Projects
- Internships
- Placements
- Higher education
- Personal guidance

---

## Does every student get a mentor?

Yes.

Students are usually assigned a mentor at the beginning of the academic year.

---

## How often should students meet mentors?

Students are encouraged to meet mentors regularly or whenever academic guidance is required.

---

## Can mentors help with internships?

Yes.

Faculty mentors guide students in selecting and completing internships.

---

## Can mentors help with placements?

Yes.

Mentors provide career guidance and placement preparation advice.

---

## Can mentors guide higher studies?

Yes.

Faculty members assist students preparing for higher education examinations and admissions.
