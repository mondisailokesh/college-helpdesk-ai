# Sports

## Does the college encourage sports activities?

Yes.

The college encourages students to participate in various indoor and outdoor sports for physical fitness, teamwork and overall personality development.

The college provides facilities for:

- Cricket
- Football
- Volleyball
- Basketball
- Badminton
- Table Tennis
- Chess
- Carrom
- Athletics
- Kabaddi
- Kho-Kho
