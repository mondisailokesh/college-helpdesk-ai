# Student Services

## What student services are available?

The college provides various student support services including:

- Student ID Card
- Bonafide Certificate
- Transfer Certificate (TC)
- Migration Certificate
- Fee Receipts
- Scholarship Assistance
- Student Counseling
- Lost and Found
- Grievance Cell
- Document Verification
- Medical Support
- Academic Support

---

## How can I get my student ID card?

Students receive their ID card after completing the admission process.

---

## What should I do if I lose my ID card?

Immediately report the loss to the Administration Office and apply for a duplicate ID card.

---

## Can I get a duplicate ID card?

Yes.

Students can apply for a duplicate ID card after submitting the required application.

---

## Is there a fee for a duplicate ID card?

Yes.

A replacement fee may be applicable according to college policy.

---

## What is a Bonafide Certificate?

A Bonafide Certificate confirms that the student is currently studying in the college.

---

## How can I apply for a Bonafide Certificate?

Students should submit a request to the Administration Office or apply through the Student Portal if available.

---

## How long does it take to receive a Bonafide Certificate?

Processing time depends on the administration office.

---

## What is a Transfer Certificate (TC)?

A Transfer Certificate is issued when a student leaves the college permanently.

---

## How do I apply for a Transfer Certificate?

Students should submit an application to the Administration Office after completing all required formalities.

---

## What is a Migration Certificate?

A Migration Certificate is required when joining another university.

---

## Can I apply for a Migration Certificate?

Yes.

Eligible students may apply through the Administration Office.

---

## What is document verification?

Document verification confirms the authenticity of academic records and certificates.

## Can the college verify my documents?

Yes.

Authorized officials verify student documents.

## Can I obtain fee receipts?

Yes.

## What should I do if my fee payment is not updated?
