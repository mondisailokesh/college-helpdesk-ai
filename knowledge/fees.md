# College Fee Structure

## Tuition Fee

The tuition fee is ₹65,000 per semester.

Students should pay the tuition fee before the semester begins.

Late fee charges may apply after the due date.

---

## Hostel Fee

The hostel fee is ₹40,000 per semester.

The hostel fee includes:

- Accommodation
- Electricity
- Water
- Maintenance

Mess charges are collected separately if applicable.

---

## Bus Fee

The bus transportation fee is ₹15,000 per semester.

The bus facility covers major nearby towns and villages.

Students must carry their bus ID card while travelling.

---

## Fee Payment Methods

Students can pay fees through:

- College ERP Portal
- Net Banking
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- UPI
- Bank Challan

---

## Fee Due Dates

Fees should be paid before the commencement of every semester.

Late payment attracts additional charges.

---

## Refund Policy

Refunds are processed according to college and university regulations.

Approved refunds are transferred to the student's registered bank account.
