# Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I get a bonafide certificate?

Visit the administration office or apply through the ERP portal.

---

## How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot Password option available on the College AI Portal.

---

## How do I raise a complaint?

Simply ask the chatbot.

Example:

"I have a problem with hostel WiFi."

The chatbot will automatically create a support ticket.

---

## How do I check my complaint status?

Ask:

"Show my tickets."

---

## Where is the examination branch?

Block A, First Floor.

---

## What is the library timing?

9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

---

## What is the minimum attendance?

75%.

---

## Can I pay fees online?

Yes.

The ERP portal supports online payments.

---

## How many books can I borrow?

Students can borrow three books for fifteen days.

---

## How do I contact the placement cell?

Ask:

"Placement cell contact."

The chatbot will provide phone number, email and office location.
