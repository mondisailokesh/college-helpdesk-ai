# Examination Rules

## How many examinations are conducted in a semester?

Students generally have:

- Internal Examinations
- Laboratory Examinations
- Mid Examinations
- Semester End Examinations

---

## What are internal examinations?

Internal examinations evaluate students during the semester through tests, assignments, quizzes and laboratory work.

---

## What is the semester examination?

The semester examination is the final university examination conducted at the end of each semester.

---

## Where can I check the examination timetable?

Students can check the timetable through:

- Student Portal
- Examination Cell
- Department Notice Board
- Official College Website

---

## How do I download my hall ticket?

Students can download their hall ticket from the Student Portal before the examination.

---

## Is the hall ticket compulsory?

Yes.

Students must carry a valid hall ticket for every examination.

---

## What documents should I carry to the examination?

Students should carry:

- Hall Ticket
- College ID Card
- Required stationery

---

## What stationery is allowed?

Students should carry:

- Blue or Black Pens
- Pencil
- Eraser
- Scale
- Calculator (only if permitted)

---

## Are electronic gadgets allowed?

No.

Mobile phones, smart watches, Bluetooth devices and other electronic gadgets are prohibited.

---

## What time should students reach the examination hall?

Students should reach at least 30 minutes before the examination starts.

---

## Can students enter after the examination starts?

Late entry depends on university and college examination rules.

---

## Can students leave early?

Students may leave only after the minimum permitted time specified by the examination rules.

---

## Is attendance compulsory for examinations?

Yes.

Only eligible students can appear for examinations.

---

## What is the minimum attendance required?

Students generally require at least 75% attendance to become eligible.

---

## What happens if attendance is below the required percentage?

Students may not be eligible to appear for the examination unless permitted by university regulations.

---

## What is malpractice?

Malpractice includes:

- Copying answers
- Possessing unauthorized material
- Using mobile phones
- Communicating with other students
- Impersonation

---

## What happens if malpractice is detected?

Strict disciplinary action is taken according to university regulations.

---

## Can students use calculators?

Calculators are permitted only if allowed for the subject.

---

## Can students bring rough papers?

No.

Rough work should be done only in the answer booklet.

---

## Can students ask for extra answer sheets?

Yes.

Additional sheets are provided when required.

---

## Can students write with different ink colors?

Students should use blue or black ink unless instructed otherwise.

---

## What happens if I miss an examination?

Students should contact the Examination Cell immediately and follow university procedures.

---

## Can I write a supplementary examination?

Yes.

Eligible students may appear for supplementary examinations.

---

## What is a backlog examination?

A backlog examination allows students to clear subjects they could not pass previously.

---

## How do I apply for supplementary examinations?

Applications are submitted through the Examination Cell within the specified dates.

---

## Can I apply for revaluation?

Yes.

Students may apply for answer script revaluation after results are announced.

---

## What is revaluation?

Revaluation is the process of rechecking the answer script for evaluation errors.

---

## Can I obtain a photocopy of my answer script?

Availability depends on university regulations.

---

## How are internal marks calculated?

Internal marks are calculated based on:

- Mid examinations
- Assignments
- Quizzes
- Laboratory performance
- Attendance (where applicable)

---

## Can internal marks be corrected?

Students should contact the concerned faculty member if any discrepancy is noticed.

---

## Where can I check examination results?

Students can view results through the Student Portal or university website.

---

## When are results announced?

Results are published after the evaluation process is completed.

---

## Can students improve their grades?

Students may improve grades according to university regulations.

---

## What is the passing criterion?

Students must obtain the minimum marks prescribed by the university.

---

## Can students challenge examination results?

Students may apply for revaluation or other permitted procedures.

---

## Are practical examinations compulsory?

Yes.

Students must complete practical examinations for laboratory subjects.

---

## Is project evaluation compulsory?

Yes.

Final-year students must complete project evaluation successfully.

---

## What happens if I lose my hall ticket?

Students should immediately contact the Examination Cell.

---

## Can parents collect hall tickets?

Generally, hall tickets are issued directly to students or through the student portal.

---

## Are exam schedules ever changed?

Yes.

Students should regularly check official announcements for updates.

---

## Can students carry bags into the examination hall?

No.

Students should leave bags outside the examination hall unless instructed otherwise.

---

## Are water bottles allowed?

Transparent water bottles are generally permitted.

---

## Is identity verification done?

Yes.

Students may be asked to show their College ID Card.

---

## What should I do before the examination?

Students should:

- Revise the syllabus
- Carry required documents
- Reach early
- Follow examination instructions

---

## What should I do after the examination?

Students should verify that all answer sheets have been submitted before leaving.

---

## Who should students contact regarding examination issues?

Students should contact:

- Examination Cell
- Department Office
- Concerned Faculty
- Academic Section

---

## Can examination fees be paid online?

Yes.

Examination fees are generally paid through the student portal or approved payment methods.

---

## Can students check fee payment status?

Yes.

Payment status is available through the student portal.

---

## Can students receive examination notifications?

Yes.

Notifications are published through:

- Student Portal
- Official Website
- Department Notice Boards
- Examination Cell

---

## What happens if a student is absent for a practical exam?

The student should contact the department and follow university regulations regarding practical examinations.

---

## Can students write examinations without paying exam fees?

No.

Eligible examination fee payment is generally mandatory before appearing for examinations.

---

## Are seating arrangements published before examinations?

Yes.

Seating arrangements are displayed before the examination.

---

## How can students prepare for examinations?

Students should:

- Attend classes regularly
- Complete assignments
- Practice previous question papers
- Revise notes
- Clarify doubts with faculty
