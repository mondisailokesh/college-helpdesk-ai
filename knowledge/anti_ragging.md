# Anti Ragging

## Does the college have an Anti-Ragging Policy?

Yes.

The college follows a strict Anti-Ragging Policy according to the guidelines of UGC and AICTE.

---

## What is ragging?

Ragging includes any act that causes physical, mental, emotional or psychological harassment to another student.

---

## Is ragging allowed?

No.

Ragging is strictly prohibited inside and outside the college campus.

---

## Is the campus ragging free?

Yes.

The college maintains a ragging-free campus through strict monitoring.

---

## What happens if a student is involved in ragging?

Strict disciplinary action is taken according to college regulations and UGC guidelines.

---

## What punishments are given?

Depending on the seriousness of the incident, punishments may include:

- Warning
- Suspension
- Fine
- Hostel suspension
- Expulsion
- Police complaint

---

## Can seniors interact with juniors?

Yes.

Healthy interaction is encouraged, but any form of harassment is prohibited.

---

## What should I do if someone is ragging me?

Immediately report the incident to:

- Anti-Ragging Committee
- Anti-Ragging Squad
- Department Head
- Faculty Mentor
- Hostel Warden
- Principal

---

## Can I complain anonymously?

Yes.

Anonymous complaints may be accepted depending on college policy.

---

## Will my identity remain confidential?

Yes.

The college maintains confidentiality whenever possible.

---

## How can I report ragging?

Students may report ragging through:

- Department Office
- Hostel Warden
- Anti-Ragging Committee
- Student Portal
- College Help Desk

---

## Is there an Anti-Ragging Committee?

Yes.

The college has an Anti-Ragging Committee responsible for preventing ragging incidents.

---

## What is the Anti-Ragging Squad?

The Anti-Ragging Squad regularly monitors the campus and hostels to prevent ragging.

---

## Does the college conduct awareness programs?

Yes.

Awareness sessions are organized every academic year.

---

## Are freshers informed about anti-ragging rules?

Yes.

Students receive anti-ragging instructions during orientation.

---

## Do students sign an anti-ragging declaration?

Yes.

Students and parents may submit anti-ragging affidavits during admission.

---

## Is ragging a criminal offense?

Yes.

Serious ragging cases may involve legal action.

---

## Can parents report ragging?

Yes.

Parents can contact the college authorities if their child faces ragging.

---

## Does hostel ragging have separate rules?

Yes.

Hostel ragging is treated very seriously and may result in hostel suspension.

---

## Can witnesses report ragging?

Yes.

Any student witnessing ragging should immediately report it.

---

## Is verbal abuse considered ragging?

Yes.

Verbal harassment and abusive language are forms of ragging.

---

## Is online ragging prohibited?

Yes.

Cyberbullying and online harassment are also treated as ragging.

---

## Does the college provide counseling to victims?

Yes.

Counseling and support are provided to affected students.

---

## Are anti-ragging posters displayed?

Yes.

Awareness posters are displayed across the campus.

---

## Who can students contact for help?

Students can contact:

- Anti-Ragging Committee
- Principal
- Hostel Warden
- Faculty Mentor
- Student Affairs Office

---

## Does the college encourage reporting?

Yes.

Students are encouraged to report ragging without fear.

---

## Are complaints investigated?

Yes.

Every complaint is investigated fairly and confidentially.

---

## Is false reporting punishable?

Knowingly submitting false complaints may result in disciplinary action.

---

## Where can I get more information?

Students should contact the Anti-Ragging Committee or Student Affairs Office.
