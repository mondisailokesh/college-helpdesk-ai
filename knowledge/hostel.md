# Hostel

## Hostel Timings

Hostel opens at 6:00 AM.

Students should return to the hostel before 9:30 PM.

Late entry is allowed only with permission from the hostel warden.

---

## Hostel Facilities

The hostel provides:

- WiFi
- RO Drinking Water
- Solar Hot Water
- Study Rooms
- Television Room
- Laundry Facility
- Indoor Games
- Security
- CCTV Surveillance
- First Aid

---

## Hostel Rules

Students should carry their ID cards.

Visitors are allowed only during permitted hours.

Ragging is strictly prohibited.

Smoking and alcohol are strictly prohibited.

Students must maintain cleanliness.

Noise should be avoided after 10 PM.

---

## Hostel WiFi

Free WiFi is available throughout the hostel.

Students should use their college login credentials.

Report connectivity issues through the College AI chatbot.

---

## Room Facilities

Each room includes:

- Cot
- Mattress
- Study Table
- Chair
- Fan
- Cupboard
- Power Sockets

---

## Hostel Complaints

Students can report:

- WiFi problems
- Water leakage
- Fan not working
- Light not working
- Electrical issues
- Cleaning issues
- Furniture damage

The chatbot automatically creates a support ticket.
