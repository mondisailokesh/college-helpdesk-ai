# Student Clubs

## Does the college have student clubs?

Yes.

The college encourages students to participate in various technical, cultural, sports, and social service clubs.

---

## Why should I join a club?

Joining a club helps students:

- Improve technical skills
- Build leadership qualities
- Improve communication skills
- Meet students from different departments
- Participate in competitions

---

## What technical clubs are available?

The college may have clubs such as:

- Coding Club
- AI & Machine Learning Club
- Robotics Club
- IoT Club
- Cyber Security Club
- Web Development Club

---

## Is there a Coding Club?

Yes.

Students interested in programming, competitive coding and software development can participate.

---

## What activities are conducted in the Coding Club?

Activities include:

- Coding contests
- Hackathons
- Workshops
- DSA practice
- Project development

---

## Is there an AI Club?

Yes.

The AI Club conducts sessions on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning.

---

## What happens in the AI Club?

Students learn:

- Python
- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- NLP
- Computer Vision
- Generative AI

---

## Is there a Robotics Club?

Yes.

Students build robotic projects and participate in robotics competitions.

---

## Is there an IoT Club?

Yes.

Students work on Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi and embedded systems.

---

## Is there a Cyber Security Club?

Yes.

Students learn ethical hacking, network security and cyber awareness.

---

## Is there a Web Development Club?

Yes.

Students learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js and backend development.

---

## Does the college have an Entrepreneurship Cell?

Yes.

The Entrepreneurship Cell encourages innovation and startup ideas.

---

## Can first-year students join clubs?

Yes.

Students from all years are encouraged to participate.

---

## Is there any membership fee?

Most student clubs do not charge membership fees.

---

## Can students join multiple clubs?

Yes.

Students may participate in more than one club.

---

## How do I join a club?

Students can register during club recruitment or contact the faculty coordinator.

---

## Who coordinates student clubs?

Each club is guided by faculty coordinators and student coordinators.

---

## How are club events announced?

Club activities are announced through:

- Notice boards
- Student portal
- WhatsApp groups
- Official social media

---

## Does the college conduct hackathons?

Yes.

Technical clubs organize hackathons and coding competitions regularly.

---

## Can students organize events?

Yes.

Student coordinators can organize events with faculty approval.

---

## Are certificates provided?

Yes.

Participation certificates are provided for many club activities.

---

## Can club participation improve placements?

Yes.

Club activities improve teamwork, communication and technical skills that help during placements.

---

## Can students showcase projects?

Yes.

Students are encouraged to demonstrate innovative projects during exhibitions and technical festivals.

---

## Are workshops organized?

Yes.

Technical workshops are conducted throughout the academic year.

---

## Are guest lectures organized?

Yes.

Industry experts and alumni frequently deliver guest lectures.

---

## Can I become a student coordinator?

Yes.

Active members may become coordinators based on performance.

---

## Can I leave a club later?

Yes.

Students may discontinue participation if necessary.

- Department Office
- Student Activity Center

---

## Are innovation projects encouraged?

## Does the college support startup ideas?

Yes.
