# College Events

## Does the college conduct events?

Yes.

The college organizes technical, cultural, sports and academic events throughout the year.

---

## What types of events are conducted?

The college conducts:

- Technical Fests
- Cultural Fests
- Sports Meets
- Workshops
- Seminars
- Guest Lectures
- Hackathons
- Project Exhibitions
- Alumni Meets
- Freshers' Day
- Farewell Day
- Annual Day

---

## What is a Technical Fest?

A Technical Fest includes coding competitions, paper presentations, project exhibitions, robotics competitions and technical workshops.

---

## What is a Cultural Fest?

A Cultural Fest includes:

- Dance
- Singing
- Music
- Drama
- Fashion Shows
- Photography
- Fine Arts
